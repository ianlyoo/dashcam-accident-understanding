S144 collision-only re-ranker v3 (E10_base rank-mean ensemble), 2026-09-27

Fit sources: 750 Nexar train positives only (0 CCD, 0 negatives). Nominal Nexar
time_of_event is not verified physical contact. No private evaluation files were
used. Positive clips without a candidate within 1 s are omitted from training only.
Members, each a NumPy-portable GBDT (same S142 config) fitted on all 750 clips:
  1. embed_ctx_contact on the S142 top-10 candidates (S142 motion+box features,
     contact dynamics, frozen DINOv2-small CLS change features, within-clip context);
  2. ctx_contact on the top-20 candidates, voting on the first 10;
  3. the S142 motion+box features alone (S142-equivalent score).
The winner is the top-10 candidate with the best mean within-clip rank percentile.
Five-fold grouped Nexar OOF hit@0.3 s: 348/750 versus S142 336/750 (selected
after comparing several variants; exploratory).

Exact S142s arms: S109 Stage1, S141 Stage3; only Stage2 collision changes for
n>310. Original S109 entry/side/evasion are preserved, except entry is clamped to
collision if necessary. All reranking failures preserve S109 per clip.

receipts: $DATA_DIR/s144_rr3/.
